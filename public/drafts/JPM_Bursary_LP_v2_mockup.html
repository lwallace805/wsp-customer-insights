JPMorgan-specific questions

The six questions we hear most.

For everything else, your advisor handles it on the call.

Am I eligible if I am part-time or a contractor? +
Full-time JPMC employees with at least 6 months of tenure are typically eligible. Part-time eligibility varies by role; contractors are generally not covered. Your advisor can confirm in two minutes once you share your role.
What if I have already used part of my Guild benefit this year? +
Your $7,500 annual cap resets each calendar year. If you have used some of it, you may still have enough remaining to cover the $4,800 tuition in full. Guild's portal shows your remaining balance after sign-in.
Are there tax implications? +
Under IRS Section 127, employers can provide up to $5,250 per year in tax-free education assistance. Amounts above that may be taxable to you as wages; your JPMC benefits team can confirm how this is handled on your W-2.
What if my manager says no? +
Approval rates are high at JPMC for credential-aligned programs. If a manager hesitates, your advisor can help reframe the request or escalate to L&D. We have not yet seen a denied request for FP&A specifically.
What if my Guild approval comes after May 11? +
Submit your Guild request before May 11 and we will honor the early-enrollment rate, even if final approval lands later. Send proof of submission to your advisor and you are set.
Can I do this if I am changing roles inside JPMC soon? +
Yes, as long as you remain a JPMC employee through cohort start. If you are unsure about an internal move, your advisor can help time the submission.
