Curriculum

8 modules. The full FP&A function, covered.

From the planning cycle and budgeting to AI tools and business partnering. Each module includes video lectures, case studies, and applied exercises.

M1
FP&A Introduction

The role of FP&A in the organization, key responsibilities, and how leading finance teams are structured.

M2
Planning Cycle & Budgeting

Annual planning frameworks, zero-based and driver-based budgeting, and operating plan construction.

M3
Forecasting

Rolling forecast design, driver trees, revenue and expense modeling, and forecast accuracy measurement.

M4
Financial Analysis

Variance analysis, KPI frameworks, management reporting, and performance review structures.

M5
Financial Modeling

Three-statement model builds, scenario modeling, sensitivity analysis, and working capital modeling.

M6
Business Partnering

Influencing without authority, translating financial insights for non-finance stakeholders, and executive communication.

M7
Reporting & Storytelling

Board and management deck construction, data visualization principles, and narrative framing of financial results.

M8
AI & the Future of FP&A

AI tools in the FP&A workflow, automation of routine analysis, and the skills that remain human-led.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who is this program designed for? +
FP&A analysts and managers looking to deepen financial modeling, forecasting, and business-partnering skills. Also strong for finance professionals moving into FP&A roles, accountants expanding into commercial finance, and consultants building financial modeling depth. A full applicant profile is available in the brochure.
What is the weekly time commitment? +
Approximately 10 hours per week. Live sessions are held one evening per week and are recorded for those who cannot attend live. The remaining time is self-paced: video lectures, case study exercises, and model builds.
Will my employer cover the tuition? +
Most likely. More than 80% of employers with 1,000+ employees offer some form of education benefit. The IRS Section 127 limit is $5,250 per year tax-free — which covers the full $4,800 tuition. An enrollment advisor can confirm whether your specific employer qualifies in about 15 minutes. Book a call above or get the brochure first if you'd like the full program details before speaking with an advisor.
What if I miss a live session? +
All live sessions are recorded and available within 24 hours of the session. You can complete the program entirely on your own schedule if needed, though participation in live sessions is encouraged.
How soon can I enroll for the June 8 cohort? +
Enrollment is open now. Early-enrollment pricing ends May 11. If you are submitting an employer reimbursement request, we recommend initiating it before May 25 to allow time for approval before the cohort starts. If your approval comes after May 11, we will honor the early rate as long as you have a submission confirmation.
