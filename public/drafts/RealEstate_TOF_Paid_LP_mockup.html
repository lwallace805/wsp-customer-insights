Curriculum at a glance

Eight weeks, from fundamentals to a capstone deal.

Real estate as an asset class. Property types, market cycles, and how capital flows through deals.
Cash flow fundamentals. Rent rolls, operating expenses, and net operating income in Excel.
Real estate financial modeling. Building acquisition and development models from scratch.
Debt and financing. Structuring loans, leverage, and their effect on returns.
Valuation and returns. Cap rates, IRR, equity multiples, and sensitivity analysis.
Partnership structures. Waterfalls, promotes, and how sponsors and investors split economics.
Development and value-add. Modeling ground-up and repositioning strategies.
Capstone case study. Underwrite and present a full deal, start to finish.
