Common questions

Quick answers. For everything else, ask Aubrey.

What if my employer doesn't offer tuition reimbursement? +
Many employers tuck education reimbursement inside "L&D" or "professional development" budgets without calling it that. If nothing is there, we offer monthly payment plans and alumni discounts. The advisor call confirms it in two minutes.
What if my manager says no? +
Manager pushback is almost always about timing, perceived business value, or budget assumptions. The free manager email template above pre-empts all three. If it still gets a no, your advisor can help reframe with a follow-up email or time the request for a better window.
Will this be taxable income on my W-2? +
Up to $5,250 in employer-paid education assistance is tax-free under IRS Section 127. Amounts above that may show up as additional wages. Your employer's payroll team handles the reporting; your advisor can flag what to ask.
Can I split between employer reimbursement and personal funds? +
Often yes. If your benefit cap covers part of tuition, you can pay the rest out of pocket or through our monthly payment plan. Your advisor maps the options based on your benefit limit.
My employer needs an invoice or quote before approving. Can you provide one? +
Yes. Use the invoice request link in the resources section above, or email your advisor directly. We'll send a formal tuition invoice within 24 hours — accepted by Guild Education, EdAssist (Bright Horizons), InStride, and all major direct-HR programs.
