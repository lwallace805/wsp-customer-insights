Common questions

Quick answers. For everything else, ask an advisor.

My employer needs an invoice before approving. How do I get one? +
Use the "Request a tuition invoice" CTA in the next-steps section above. We send a formal invoice within 24 hours with Net 30 terms. You'll also be pre-enrolled immediately so you can access prerequisite coursework while the invoice moves through your finance team. Accepted by Guild Education, EdAssist (Bright Horizons), InStride, and direct HR programs.
My employer is ready to pay. Can you send them a payment link? +
Yes. Use the "Have approval already?" link in the next-steps section. You'll get a direct payment link your manager or HR can click to charge their card or process payment without going through the invoice flow.
What if my employer doesn't offer tuition reimbursement? +
Many employers tuck education reimbursement inside "L&D" or "professional development" budgets without calling it that. If nothing is there, we offer 5 monthly installments of $1,000 and approximately $550 in tuition assistance available via application. The advisor call confirms options in two minutes.
What if my manager says no? +
Manager pushback is almost always about timing, perceived business value, or budget assumptions. The manager email template above pre-empts all three. If it still gets a no, your advisor can help reframe with a follow-up email or time the request for a better window.
Will this be taxable income on my W-2? +
Up to $5,250 in employer-paid education assistance is tax-free under IRS Section 127. Amounts above that may show up as additional wages. Your employer's payroll team handles the reporting; your advisor can flag what to ask.
Can I split between employer reimbursement and personal funds? +
Often yes. If your benefit cap covers part of tuition, you can pay the rest out of pocket or through our 5-monthly-installment plan ($1,000/month). Your advisor maps the options based on your benefit limit.

Manager approval email · Template

Copy and send. Pre-populated with this program's details.

Adapt to your voice and situation. Confident but not presumptuous; you're making a case, not submitting an expense report.

Subject: Professional Development Opportunity — Quick Question Hi [Manager's Name], Following our conversations about how I can continue growing in my role, I've been looking into professional development programs and wanted to run one by you. I recently came across the Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Certificate Program offered by Wharton Online and Wall Street Prep, and it stood out to me because [briefly explain why; relevance to your role, a skill it develops, your career trajectory]. I believe it would directly benefit our team by [one specific, concrete example; e.g., "strengthening my ability to lead the budgeting cycle" or "deepening my financial modeling capabilities"]. I reviewed our tuition reimbursement policy and believe this program qualifies under [e.g., "the professional development benefit" / "the annual education assistance allowance"]. I've put together the program details below to help make the approval process as straightforward as possible: • Program: Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Certificate Program • Institution: Wharton Online + Wall Street Prep • Cost: $5,000 • Duration: 8 weeks (June 8 to August 2, 2026) • Start date: June 8, 2026 • More info: View program page Would you have 15 minutes to discuss this week? If there's a specific form or approval process I should follow, I'm happy to take care of that on my end. Thanks so much. Best, [Your Name]
