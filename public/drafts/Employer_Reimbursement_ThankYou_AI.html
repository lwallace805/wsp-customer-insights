Common questions

Quick answers. For everything else, ask an advisor.

My employer needs an invoice before approving. How do I get one? +
Use the "Request a tuition invoice" CTA above. We send a formal invoice within 24 hours with Net 30 terms. You'll also be pre-enrolled immediately so you can access prerequisite coursework while the invoice moves through your finance team. Accepted by Guild Education, EdAssist (Bright Horizons), InStride, and direct HR programs.
My manager needs an invoice before approving. How do I get one? +
Use the "Request a tuition invoice" CTA above. We send a formal invoice within 24 hours with Net 30 terms. You'll also be pre-enrolled immediately so you can access prerequisite coursework while the invoice moves through your finance team. Accepted by Guild Education and JPMorgan L&D.
My employer is ready to pay. Can you send them a payment link? +
Yes. Use the "Have approval already?" link above. You'll get a direct payment link your manager or HR can click to charge their card or process payment without going through the invoice flow.
My manager is ready to pay. Can you send them a payment link? +
Yes. Use the "Already approved?" link above. You'll get a direct payment link your manager or HR can click to charge their card or process payment without going through the invoice flow.
What if my employer doesn't offer tuition reimbursement? +
Many employers tuck education reimbursement inside "L&D" or "professional development" budgets without calling it that. If nothing is there, we offer 5 monthly installments and approximately $550 in tuition assistance available via application. The advisor call confirms options in two minutes.
What if my JPMorgan L&D benefit doesn't cover the full tuition? +
If your JPM L&D allowance is partial, we offer 5 monthly installments and approximately $550 in tuition assistance available via application. The advisor call confirms your options in two minutes.
What if my manager says no? +
Manager pushback is almost always about timing, perceived business value, or budget assumptions. The manager email template above pre-empts all three. If it still gets a no, your advisor can help reframe with a follow-up email or time the request for a better window.

Most large US employers use one of four channels.

If you know which one yours uses, you're 10 minutes from a submitted reimbursement request.

1Guild Education
JPMorgan Chase, Walmart, Disney, Chipotle, Starbucks, Discover, Lowe's

Bank of America, Citi, PwC, IBM, Verizon
Look for "EdAssist" inside your benefits portal.
3InStride
Adobe, Uber, Anheuser-Busch, Caesars
Search "InStride" inside your HR portal.
4Direct HR
Most midsize firms; many financial services and professional services employers
