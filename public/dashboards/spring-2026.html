Brand SEM: spend up, enrollments flat

Brand SEM consumed $99K in Spring 2026 vs $58K in Winter 2026 (+70%) for 25 enrollments vs 26. CPE up 77% ($2,244 → $3,970). Brenden's #1 recommendation: cut to Winter daily spend rates immediately, reallocate ~$40K to ROAS-positive campaigns.

Google PMax: PE and RE click-quality collapse

PE Google clicks -76% CoC on -25% spend cut. RE clicks -2% but leads -64% (click-to-lead fell from 5.9% to 2.2%). Brenden flags this as the "PMax Black Box Issue." Audit asset groups, audience signals, brand exclusions before adding more spend.
